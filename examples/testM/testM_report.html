7. Methods

VNTR amplicons were located in the assemblies with mlvatyper 0.3.0 (https://github.com/FOI-Bioinformatics/mlvatyper) using merPCR 1.0.5, a Python reimplementation of me-PCR, allowing 2 mismatches per primer outside the 1 protected 3' base(s), a hash word of 11 bases and a product size window of the expected size plus or minus 500 bp. IUPAC ambiguity codes in primers were matched to the bases they stand for. Markers without a hit were searched again with a hash word of 6 bases, one-sided search and a window of 1000 bp, and then by anchored alignment allowing one indel in a primer. Overlapping hits for one locus were collapsed to the hit with the fewest primer mismatches. Repeat numbers were calculated as reference repeats plus the difference between the amplicon length and the reference size divided by the unit length, with reference values taken from sample test. Values within 0.25 of an integer were assigned that integer; other values were handled with the intermediate policy. Each call was cross-checked against a tandem array count tolerating 1 mismatch(es) per unit.
